# Budgets & Reports

Better Personal Finance provides monthly budgets and a suite of charts and exports to understand your finances at a glance.

---

## Budgets

### Create a budget

1. Go to **Budgets** in the sidebar.
2. Select a month using the month/year picker (defaults to the current month).
3. Click **Edit budget**.
4. For each category you want to limit, enter a monthly **budget amount**.
5. Click **Save**.

### View budget progress

The budget page shows a progress bar for each category:

- **Green** — spending is under 80 % of the budget
- **Amber** — spending is between 80 % and 100 %
- **Red** — over budget

The totals row at the bottom shows overall income vs. expenses for the month.

### Copy last month's budget

If your monthly budget is similar month-to-month, click **Copy from last month** at the top of the budget page. All budget lines from the previous month are duplicated into the current month, which you can then adjust.

### Delete a budget line

Click the **trash** icon next to a category budget line to remove it for that month.

---

## Reports

Go to **Reports** in the sidebar for an overview of your financial trends.

### Monthly income vs. expenses

A bar chart comparing total income and total expenses side-by-side for the last 12 months. Quickly identify months where you overspent.

### Category breakdown

A donut chart of expenses by category for the selected date range. Hover over a segment to see exact amounts and percentages.

### Net worth over time

A line chart of your net worth (sum of all account balances) calculated at the end of each month. Tracks your wealth-building progress.

### 12-month cash flow

A stacked bar chart showing income, expenses, and net cash flow each month. Positive net = savings that month.

### Filters

Use the date range picker and account selector at the top of the Reports page to narrow data to specific periods or accounts.

---

## CSV Export

1. Go to **Reports**.
2. Select the desired date range and account filters.
3. Click **Export CSV**.
4. A file is downloaded containing every transaction matching the filters with columns:

```
date, account, payee, category, amount, currency, type, notes
```

The export is suitable for importing into Excel, LibreOffice Calc, or any accounting tool.

---

## Tips

- Set budgets at subcategory level for fine-grained control (e.g. "Groceries" separate from "Restaurants").
- Check the **monthly income vs. expenses** chart weekly to catch overspend early.
- Use **net worth over time** as your primary long-term progress indicator.
